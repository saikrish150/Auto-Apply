System Architecture & How It Works Locally

Complete technical blueprint of your local AI Job Application system.

1

Local Chrome Integration

The agent connects directly to your installed Google Chrome browser on your local machine using Chrome's native Remote Debugging Protocol (CDP port 9222). Because it runs on your real machine with your real browser session, your existing logins, cookies, and 2FA for LinkedIn and Naukri are preserved seamlessly with zero passwords stored in code.

2

Search & Filtering

The orchestrator navigates to LinkedIn (Easy Apply filter) and Naukri. It extracts job postings, descriptions, required qualifications, and salary disclosures directly from the DOM.

3

AI ATS Synergy Evaluation

The AI Agent analyzes the job description against your candidate profile in real time. It computes a match score (0-100%). If the job does not meet your threshold (e.g. 65%), it skips it to protect your application history from irrelevant roles.

4

Hybrid AI Screening Q&A

Uses a two-tier intelligence system: ⚡ Local pattern matching answers known questions instantly (CTC, notice period, experience years, yes/no) with zero API calls. For complex or unknown questions, it 🤖 escalates to Google Gemini AI which has your full profile context loaded and generates intelligent, contextual answers in real time.

5

Safety & Human-in-the-Loop

With Review Before Submit (Semi-Auto), you can inspect the filled application on your screen before clicking send. If a CAPTCHA appears, you can solve it directly in your Chrome window!

6

Local Privacy Guarantee

Everything runs strictly on http://localhost:3000. No candidate data is sent to third-party databases. You have 100% ownership and control over your session.
